# Investment scams

Investment scams promise guaranteed, unrealistic returns to lure deposits. They include ponzi schemes, pump-and-dump groups, fake trading platforms, and 'guaranteed' forex/crypto panels.

## The universal warning signs

1. **Guaranteed returns** - real investing has risk; by definition, nobody can guarantee returns. "Guaranteed 5x in 6 months" is mathematically a scam.
2. **Too-good numbers** - doubling money monthly, 50% weekly, 'minimum risk, maximum profit'.
3. **Urgency** - "investment window closes today", "only 10 seats left".
4. **Recruitment-based bonuses** - "refer friends and earn commission" (ponzi structure).
5. **Pressure to go fast and quiet** - 'act alone, tell no one, hurry'.
6. **Non-standard payment rails** - payments to personal accounts, gift cards, crypto wallets.

## Common investment scam templates

- "Double your money in 30 days with our guaranteed crypto signals."
- "Forex trading made easy - Rs.10,000 minimum returns monthly."
- "Our expert panel will trade for you. Minimum investment Rs.50,000."
- "Win guaranteed monthly income by staking your crypto."
- "Stock tips from a SEBI-registered guru - join the paid WhatsApp group (Rs.2,000/month)."

## The pyramid structure

The mathematical problem with guaranteed-return schemes is that they can only pay early investors with money from later investors. The scheme grows until new investors stop arriving, then collapses - almost always with the mastermind's money taken out. This is why they recruit aggressively and pay "referral bonuses".

## Detection notes

Investment vocabulary (returns, profit, crypto, trading signals, double your money) is a high-severity indicator in the engine. Wrapped in urgency plus a registration fee, such a message is scored HIGH. The RAG context for investment scam explanations emphasizes the guarantee-tells above, because no legitimate instrument guarantees returns.
