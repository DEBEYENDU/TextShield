# Job scams

Job scams advertise attractive jobs - work from home, data entry, telesales, modeling, overseas placement - and charge money for the privilege of being hired. Nobody legitimate does that.

## Why job scams recruit so well

- Unemployment and underemployment make "easy jobs" desperately attractive.
- The promised income (Rs.50,000/month, Rs.25,000/week) is set just above the victim's reach.
- The work described (data entry, liking videos, captcha typing) sounds human but trivial.
- Fees (Rs.300-Rs.2,500) are low enough to count as 'not a real loss' at the moment of payment.

## Typical job scam templates

- "Earn Rs.50,000 per month from home. No experience required. Pay Rs.999 registration fee to start."
- "Data entry jobs available - Rs.200 per hour. Message to apply."
- "Your resume has been shortlisted by a multinational. Pay Rs.2,000 for the interview slot."
- "Part-time job: like videos on social media and earn daily salary."
- "Guaranteed job placement after our 5-day paid training program."

## The tell-tale pattern

Legitimate hiring is reversed: employers pay you, and they never charge job-seekers money. Any of these details - registration fee, training fee, interview fee, deposit, document-processing fee, 'refundable' security - marks the offer as a scam.

## Extended variants

- **Task or 'earning app' scams**: you complete fake tasks and get paid small amounts to build trust, then are asked to 'invest' more to unlock bigger task batches - the money disappears at the first large deposit.
- **Identity theft variants**: asking for PAN/Aadhaar 'for payroll' before any legitimate company relationship exists.

## Detection notes

The job-scam indicator (work-from-home, earn-per-day, fee language) is high severity in TextShield. Combined with a payment request and registration-fee wording, the message is scored HIGH, and the RAG layer retrieves job-scam documentation to support the explanation.
