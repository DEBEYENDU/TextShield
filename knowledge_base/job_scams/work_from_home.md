# Fake work-from-home opportunities

"Work from home" is the most impersonated job category because the scam works entirely online: no interview room, no physical office, no paperwork - nothing exposes the fraud until the fees are paid.

## The classic WFH scam ladder

1. **Bait** - "Earn Rs.15,000 - Rs.50,000 per month from home. No experience required."
2. **First fee** - "Register with Rs.300 to receive your task kit."
3. **Second fee** - "Complete the premium training module for Rs.1,200 to unlock daily tasks."
4. **Third fee** - "Activate your earnings dashboard - one-time setup, refundable after 30 days."
5. **Disappearance** - after the last payment the 'company' stops replying.

## Variants worth knowing

- **Task scam (prepaid tasks)** - earning apps pay small amounts for early tasks, then demand 'investment' to unlock higher-value task batches. Victims lose the whole deposit.
- **Liking/subscribing jobs** - 'like videos, subscribe channels, get paid' - same escalation pattern.
- **Kit refill premium** - 'buy the starter kit, do the work, we buy back results' - the kit never qualifies.
- **Resume for a fee** - 'guaranteed placement' at a MNC after paying for CV polishing/interview training.

## Legitimacy checklist

- No legitimate employer charges job-seekers any money - ever.
- Genuine remote jobs publish details: company registered, tax number, written contract, named recruiter verifiable on LinkedIn/company site.
- The employer's banking is corporate, never a personal account.



## Detection notes

"Earn X per month from home" + a fee + urgency is TextShield's classic HIGH-risk constellation. The recommended action for any such message: delete, do not transfer money, and warn family members who are job-hunting.
