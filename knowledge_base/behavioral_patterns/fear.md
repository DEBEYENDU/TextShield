Title: Fear
Category: behavioral_patterns
Summary: A manipulation technique that exploits anxiety, dread, or panic to prompt immediate action.
Description: Fear-based manipulation creates imagined or exaggerated threats that prompt victims to act quickly to avoid perceived negative consequences. Scammers amplify real concerns or fabricate threats to trigger fear responses, bypassing critical thinking. Legitimate uses include genuine safety warnings, emergency alerts, and health advisories.
Typical Scenario: "Your computer has a virus!" or "Your account will be closed!" or "You owe taxes or face arrest!" or "Your family is in danger!"
Purpose: Provoked fear response; bypass critical thinking; extract resources or information through threat avoidance
Detection Signals: Exaggerated or fabricated threats; consequences for inaction; emotional language designed to frighten; urgency combined with fear; unverifiable danger claims
Legitimate Uses: Genuine safety warnings; emergency alerts; health advisories; real threat notifications
Examples: "Your account will be suspended," "Viruses detected on your device," "Unusual login attempt - secure now"
Risk Level: High - fear is one of the most powerful manipulation techniques
References: FTC Fear-Based Scam Reports; Psychology of Fear Appeals; APWG Fear Tactics in Phishing Data
