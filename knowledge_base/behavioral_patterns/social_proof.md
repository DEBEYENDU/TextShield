Title: Social Proof
Category: behavioral_patterns
Summary: A manipulation technique that exploits the tendency to follow the actions or decisions of others.
Description: Social Proof manipulation creates illusion of widespread acceptance or participation to prompt victims to join or comply. Scammers fabricate testimonials, fake reviews, false membership numbers, or staged scenarios to suggest others are participating or benefiting. Legitimate uses include genuine customer reviews, real popularity metrics, and actual community participation.
Typical Scenario: "Everyone is investing in this" or "Join 10,000 other satisfied customers" or "Most people approve this" or "Our most popular plan"
Purpose: Create perception of correctness; exploit desire to conform; prompt action based on false consensus
Detection Signals: Fabricated testimonials; inflated participation numbers; staged reviews; " everyone is doing it" without verification; popularity claims without proof
Legitimate Uses: Genuine customer reviews; real popularity metrics; actual community participation; verified purchase ratings
Examples: "Join 50,000 satisfied users" "Best-selling product" "Most reviewed in category" "Customer favorite - 4.5 stars"
Risk Level: Medium - effective on those uncertain or seeking social guidance
