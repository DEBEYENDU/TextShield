Title: Reward
Category: behavioral_patterns
Summary: A manipulation technique that promises benefits or gains to prompt desired actions.
Description: Reward exploitation promises desirable outcomes (money, prizes, status, access) to motivate victims to provide information, pay fees, or perform actions. The reward may be fake, exaggerated, or conditional on completing requested actions. Legitimate uses include genuine promotions, loyalty programs, and actual rewards.
Typical Scenario: "You've won $1,000!" or "Exclusive access available" or "Earn up to 20% returns" or "Free gift card for completing survey"
Purpose: Motivate action through promise of gain; engage victim; extract resources or information
Detection Signals: "Too good to be true" promises; requests required to claim reward; conditional gain; exclusive or limited access claims; payment required to receive reward
Legitimate Uses: Genuine promotions; loyalty rewards; actual prizes; referral programs; legitimate bonuses
Examples: "Survey winner - claim your prize," "Refer a friend - earn $50," "Loyalty points redemption," "Exclusive investment opportunity"
Risk Level: Medium to High - reward exploitation effective on desire for gain
