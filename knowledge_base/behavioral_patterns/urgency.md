Title: Urgency
Category: behavioral_patterns
Summary: A manipulation technique that creates artificial time pressure to prompt immediate action without proper verification.
Description: Urgency exploits the human tendency to respond quickly to perceived deadlines or imminent threats. Scammers create fake expiration dates, limited-time offers, or immediate consequences to bypass critical thinking. Legitimate uses include genuine sales promotions, event registrations, and real emergency notifications.
Typical Scenario: "Your account will be suspended in 2 hours!" or "Limited time offer ends at midnight!" or "Transfer funds immediately to avoid penalties"
Purpose: Prompt quick decision-making; bypass verification; extract resources before victim realizes the scam
Detection Signals: Artificial time pressure; consequences for inaction; "act now or else" messaging; no option to verify independently; urgency without legitimate cause
Legitimate Uses: Genuine sales with clear end dates; event registration deadlines; real emergency alerts; time-sensitive service updates
Examples: "Holiday sale ends tonight," "Subscription about to auto-renew," "Security alert - act within 24 hours"
Risk Level: High - one of the most commonly used scam techniques
