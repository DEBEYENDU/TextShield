Title: Trust Building
Category: behavioral_patterns
Summary: A manipulation technique that establishes false trust over time to lower victim resistance.
Description: Trust Building manipulation gradually establishes credibility and rapport over extended interaction to make victims more comfortable sharing information, sending money, or granting access. Scammers invest time in relationship-building, credibility fabrication, or context establishment before making their request. Legitimate uses include genuine relationship development, business networking, and established trust in long-term partnerships.
Typical Scenario: "I've been talking to you for weeks/months" or "Our company has worked together for years" or "I've verified your account details" or "Building on our previous successful interaction"
Purpose: Lower victim resistance; establish credibility; make subsequent requests seem legitimate; extract resources through established trust
Detection Signals: Rapid relationship progression; unverifiable history; consistent story over time; gradual request escalation; emotional investment before ask; "we've worked together before" without proof
Legitimate Uses: Genuine relationship development; business networking; established long-term partnerships; verified account histories
Examples: "We've been customers for years" "Our previous successful transaction" "I remember our conversation last month" "Based on your account history"
Risk Level: Medium to High - effective over extended periods; harder to detect than acute tactics
