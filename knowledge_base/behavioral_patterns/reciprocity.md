Title: Reciprocity
Category: behavioral_patterns
Summary: A manipulation technique that exploits the social norm of returning favors or benefits.
Description: Reciprocity-based manipulation provides something small or seemingly generous to create a sense of obligation in the victim, who then feels compelled to return the favor - often by providing money, information, or compliance. Scammers offer "free" gifts, trials, or favors that later require reciprocation. Legitimate uses include genuine free trials, sample offerings, and legitimate loyalty programs.
Typical Scenario: "Free trial that auto-enrolls" or "Gift card you've won" or "Someone sent you a gift" or "Free assessment - no obligation"
Purpose: Create obligation; exploit social norm; prompt compliance or resource extraction through felt debt
Detection Signals: Unsolicited "gift" or "favor"; later request for reciprocation; guilt-tripping if declining; "free" that later requires payment; obligation-creating without legitimate basis
Legitimate Uses: Genuine free trials; sample products; loyalty benefits; legitimate referral programs; legitimate gifts
Examples: "Free trial - cancel anytime" "Gift card waiting for you" "Someone shared this with you" "Free assessment offered"
Risk Level: Medium - reciprocity effective on those sensitive to social norms
