Title: Scarcity
Category: behavioral_patterns
Summary: A manipulation technique that creates artificial shortage to prompt immediate action.
Description: Scarcity exploits the human tendency to value things more when they appear limited or unavailable. Scammers create fake limited quantities, time-limited access, or exclusive offers to prompt quick decisions without due diligence. Legitimate uses include genuine limited-edition products, real sales, and actual capacity constraints.
Typical Scenario: "Only 3 left at this price!" or "Sale ends in 2 hours!" or "Exclusive offer for first 10 applicants" or "Limited seating available"
Purpose: Create perceived value through artificial limitation; prompt quick decision-making; extract resources before victim realizes the scarcity is fake
Detection Signals: Artificial limitations without verification; "last chance" messaging; exclusive claims without proof; urgency combined with limited availability; no option to revisit decision
Legitimate Uses: Genuine limited-edition products; real sales with clear end dates; actual capacity constraints; pre-orders with limited slots
Examples: "Flash sale - today only!" "Only 2 seats remaining" "Members-only access" "Limited quantity available"
Risk Level: Medium to High - scarcity effective especially with urgency combination
