Title: Pressure
Category: behavioral_patterns
Summary: A manipulation technique that applies continuous coercion to force compliance.
Description: Pressure-based manipulation applies sustained pressure through repeated requests, threats, or consequences to compel compliance regardless of victim comfort. Scammers use persistent contact, escalating demands, or consequences for non-cooperation. Legitimate uses include genuine urgent deadlines, genuine consequences for action/inaction, and legitimate enforcement within legal bounds.
Typical Scenario: "I'll call you every hour until you comply" or "You must decide now or lose the opportunity" or "Failure to respond will result in..." or "Multiple follow-up messages demanding action"
Purpose: Force compliance through relentless pressure; exhaust victim resistance; extract resources before pushback possible
Detection Signals: Repeated contact without new information; escalating demands; consequences for non-compliance; refusal to accept refusal; persistence beyond reasonable bounds; pressure without legitimate cause
Legitimate Uses: Genuine urgent deadlines; legitimate enforcement; reasonable follow-up; legitimate consequences for action/inaction
Examples: "Final notice - respond within 24 hours" "Multiple follow-up calls" "Escalating demands" "Cannot proceed without response"
Risk Level: High - pressure can overcome resistance; especially effective on those avoiding conflict
