Title: Personalization
Category: behavioral_patterns
Summary: A manipulation technique that uses personal information to appear legitimate and increase compliance.
Description: Personalization manipulation incorporates victim's name, personal details, or specific context to appear legitimate and bypass skepticism. Scammers obtain personal data through breaches, social media, or prior scams, then use it to create false familiarity. Legitimate uses include genuine personalized communication, legitimate customer service, and context-aware legitimate interactions.
Typical Scenario: "We know your name is John and you recently purchased..." or "Based on your account, you're eligible for..." or "John, your subscription is expiring..." or "We see you've been a customer since 2020"
Purpose: Establish false legitimacy; bypass verification; increase compliance through apparent knowledge; make scam appear personalized rather than mass-marketed
Detection Signals: Personal information used without legitimate context; details from data breaches presented as current knowledge; "personalized" offers too broad; information doesn't match actual relationship; personal details used to appear credible
Legitimate Uses: Genuine customer service with account history; legitimate personalized offers; legitimate account communications; context-aware interactions
Examples: "John, your account is expiring" "Based on your purchase history" "We see you live at Apt 5" "John, we noticed your interest in..."
Risk Level: Medium - personalization makes scams appear more credible but often contains subtle errors
