Title: Mixed Intent Example
Category: examples
Summary: Example of mixed intent communication - tech support pop-up with genuine and fake elements.
Original Message: Pop-up appearing as: "Your computer has 3 viruses! Click here to scan or call 1-800-555-0199 for immediate support. Your system will crash in 5 minutes if not fixed. McAfee Security Alert."
Expected Intent: Mixed - could be genuine detection or fake scareware
Expected Behavior: Victim may click link/call number; may download actual malware or pay for unnecessary support
Expected Manipulation: Fear (viruses detected), Urgency (5-minute crash), Authority (claimed McAfee), Scarcity (imminent crash)
Reasoning: Pop-up mimics genuine security software formatting; claims specific virus count (3); creates immediate crisis (5-minute crash); displays phone number for "support"; may be genuine malware detection or fake scareware designed to extract money; hard to distinguish without further investigation
Expected Risk: Medium to High - scareware can lead to malware installation or financial loss
