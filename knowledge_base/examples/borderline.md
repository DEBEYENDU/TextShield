Title: Borderline Example
Category: examples
Summary: Example of ambiguous communication that could be legitimate or scam - subscription renewal notice.
Original Message: "Subject: Important: Your Netflix membership requires update Sent: noreply@netflix-update.net Dear John, Your Netflix membership is set to renew soon. To continue uninterrupted service, please update your payment method. Update now or your membership will pause. http://netflix-pay-update.net Update Payment Method"
Expected Intent: Mixed - could be legitimate renewal notice or fake update demand
Expected Behavior: Victim may update payment method thinking it's legitimate, or may realize it's fake
Expected Manipulation: Urgency (membership will pause), Authority (claimed Netflix), Social Proof (many users), Personalization (name "John")
Reasoning: Message has some legitimacy (Netflix branding, personal name, real service context) but domain is slightly altered (netflix-update.net vs netflix.com); urgency tactic; request to update payment method; could be genuine renewal notice or scam - depends on whether recipient has Netflix and recent activity
Expected Risk: Medium - borderline between legitimate service notification and phishing for payment details
