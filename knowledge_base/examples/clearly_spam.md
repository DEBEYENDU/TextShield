Title: Clearly Spam Example
Category: examples
Summary: Example of obvious spam - unsolicited promotional message with poor grammar.
Original Message: "CLICK NOW!!! FREE iPhone 15 PRO MAX!!! Limited time only!!! No purchase necessary!!! www.free-iphone-scam.com"
Expected Intent: Financial gain through deception
Expected Behavior: Victim may click link or attempt to claim non-existent prize
Expected Manipulation: Urgency (limited time), Greed (free valuable item), Authority (implied), Scarcity (exclusive offer)
Reasoning: Message uses excessive punctuation and caps; promises unrealistic reward (free iPhone); requests nothing specifically but entices click; domain appears fake; no personalization; clear indicators of spam
Expected Risk: High - classic prize scam pattern
