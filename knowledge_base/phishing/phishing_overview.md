# Phishing overview

Phishing (also called vishing over the phone, smishing over SMS) is the attempt to trick a victim into revealing credentials, OTPs, card numbers or personal identity data by impersonating a trusted organization.

## How a phishing attack unfolds

1. **Bait** - the victim receives an urgent message: account blocked, payment failed, prize won, package stuck.
2. **Hook** - the message asks the victim to "verify", "update" or "claim" via a link or a phone call.
3. **Harvest** - the fake page or call center collects passwords, OTPs, card details, Aadhaar/PAN numbers.
4. **Abuse** - stolen data is used for identity theft, money transfer or resale.

## Classic phishing phrases

- "Verify your account immediately"
- "Your account has been locked / suspended / blocked"
- "Update your payment details"
- "We detected unusual activity"
- "Confirm your identity to avoid deactivation"
- "KYC update required"

## Red flags to look for

- Generic greeting instead of your real name
- Shortened or lookalike URLs
- Request for OTPs - no real bank ever asks for your OTP
- Threats of blocking or legal action
- Sender address that does not match the brand domain

## Why phishing is dangerous

Phishing messages bypass naive filters because they imitate legitimate communication closely. They are the bridge between spam and fraud: a message can be technically inoffensive yet still be a phishing attempt. Detection therefore needs layering: ML classification, indicator rules, URL analysis and knowledge retrieval together produce a risk estimate no single layer could provide.
