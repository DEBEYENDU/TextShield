Title: Account Verification Scam
Category: scams
Subcategory: technical_support
Summary: Fraudulent communications claiming account verification is needed to avoid suspension.
Description: Account verification scams mimic legitimate services (bank, email, social media) claiming the victim's account will be suspended, locked, or compromised unless verification is completed immediately. Victims are directed to fake login pages or asked to provide personal information via phone, email, or SMS.
Typical Scenario: "Your account will be suspended in 24 hours - verify now" or "Unusual login detected - secure your account immediately"
Intent: Credential theft and account compromise
Behavior: Victim provides credentials or personal information; account accessed by scammer
Manipulation Techniques: Fear (account loss), Urgency (immediate action required), Authority (claimed service provider)
Common Indicators: Unsolicited account verification request; threats of account suspension; link to non-official login page; poor grammar/spelling; generic greetings
Legitimate Alternatives: Official account verification via service's app or website; known sender communications; two-factor prompts you initiated
False Positives: Actual account security alerts from services you use
False Negatives: Victims who ignore legitimate security notifications
Real-world Examples: "Bank account verification" phishing, "Email security update" scams, "Social media login required" fraud
References: APWG Account Takeover Reports; FTC Phishing Data; Service-specific security guides
