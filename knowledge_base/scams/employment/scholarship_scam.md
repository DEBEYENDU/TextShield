Title: Scholarship Scam
Category: scams
Subcategory: employment
Summary: Fraudulent offers of scholarships or grants requiring payment to apply or secure funding.
Description: Scholarship scams target students and parents with promises of guaranteed scholarships, grants, or financial aid. Victims are asked to pay application fees, processing charges, or "search service" fees. Legitimate scholarships never require payment to apply.
Typical Scenario: "Guaranteed $10,000 scholarship - pay $199 application fee" or "You've been selected for a scholarship - pay $50 for processing."
Intent: Financial gain from students seeking education funding
Behavior: Victim pays fees for non-existent or unsubstantiated scholarships
Manipulation Techniques: Greed (education funding), Authority (claimed scholarship committee), Urgency (limited application window)
Common Indicators: Guaranteed scholarship without application; fee required to apply; pressure to decide quickly; "no essay required" claims
Legitimate Alternatives: Actual scholarship applications through official education channels; free FAFSA assistance; merit-based awards from recognized institutions
False Positives: Legitimate scholarship programs with minimal application fees (rare)
False Negatives: Students who don't apply due to fear of scams
Real-world Examples: "Guaranteed student grant" calls, "University selection" email scams
References: Federal Student Aid Scam Alerts; College Board Scam Reports
