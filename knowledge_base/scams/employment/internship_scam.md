Title: Internship Scam
Category: scams
Subcategory: employment
Summary: Fraudulent internship opportunities targeting students and recent graduates.
Description: Internship scams offer course credit or experience-focused positions that require payment for "application processing," "materials," "software access," or "certification." Some involve fraudulent "virtual internships" where victims complete work but never receive credit or payment, or pay for meaningless certificates.
Typical Scenario: "Summer internship program - pay $200 for course credit and materials" or "Selected for internship - provide SSN and pay for background check."
Intent: Financial gain from students seeking experience; personal information collection
Behavior: Victim pays fees or reveals SSN/financial info for non-existent internship
Manipulation Techniques: Authority (university/claimed partnership), Urgency (limited summer positions), Greed (experience/college credit)
Common Indicators: Payment required for "internship"; no formal application process; promises of college credit without university approval; requests for SSN early
Legitimate Alternatives: Genuine internships through university career services; paid internships from recognized companies; volunteer experience opportunities
False Positives: Legitimate internship programs with minimal administrative fees (rare, usually credited/tuition-based)
False Negatives: Students who avoid legitimate internship opportunities due to scam awareness
Real-world Examples: "Virtual internship kit fee" scams, "College credit internship" fraud, "Remote unpaid labor" exploitation
References: National Association of Colleges and Employers (NACE) Scam Alerts; Student Legal Service Guides
