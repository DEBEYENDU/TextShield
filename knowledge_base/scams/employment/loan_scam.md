Title: Loan Scam
Category: scams
Subcategory: employment
Summary: Fraudulent loan offers requiring upfront fees or personal information from borrowers.
Description: Loan scams offer guaranteed loans, low-interest rates, or credit approval regardless of credit history. Victims are asked to pay "origination fees," "insurance premiums," or "processing charges" before receiving the loan. The loan never materializes, and the fee is lost.
Typical Scenario: "Guaranteed approval - pay $200 origination fee for $5,000 loan" or "Bad credit accepted - no upfront fees required (but insurance premium needed)."
Intent: Financial gain through upfront fee extraction
Behavior: Victim pays fees; loan never funded
Manipulation Techniques: Greed (access to credit), Authority (claimed lender), Urgency (limited time offer)
Common Indicators: Guaranteed approval regardless of credit; upfront fee required; lender not registered in your state; pressure to pay quickly
Legitimate Alternatives: Licensed lenders; credit unions; legitimate loan comparison services; FHA/VA loans
False Positives: Legitimate lenders with legitimate origination fees (deducted from loan, not paid upfront)
False Negatives: Borrowers discouraged from legitimate loan options
Real-world Examples: "Payday loan advance fee" scams, "Credit builder loan" fraud
References: CFPB Loan Scam Reports; FTC Credit Loan Scam Data
