Title: Insurance Scam
Category: scams
Subcategory: employment
Summary: Fraudulent insurance policies, claims, or enrollment schemes targeting victims.
Description: Insurance scams involve selling fake policies, claiming existing policies are expired requiring re-enrollment, or filing fraudulent insurance claims. Victims pay premiums for policies that provide no coverage, or scammers file claims using stolen identities.
Typical Scenario: "Affordable health insurance - enroll now or face penalty" or "Your auto insurance requires immediate verification" or "File your insurance claim - pay us 10% to process."
Intent: Financial gain through fake policies or claim fees
Behavior: Victim pays premiums for non-existent coverage or pays fake claim processing fees
Manipulation Techniques: Fear (penalty/gap in coverage), Authority (insurance company), Urgency (enrollment deadline)
Common Indicators: Unsolicited insurance contact; policies that seem too good to be true; requests for payment via wire/gift cards; no policy documentation provided
Legitimate Alternatives: Purchasing through licensed agents; verified health insurance marketplaces; existing policy documentation
False Positives: Legitimate insurance communications about policy changes or renewals
False Negatives: Policyholders who miss genuine insurer communications
Real-world Examples: "Fake health insurance" schemes, "Claim settlement fee" scams, "Auto insurance verification" phishing
References: NAIC Insurance Fraud Reports; State Insurance Department Alerts
