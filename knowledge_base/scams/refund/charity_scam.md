Title: Charity Scam
Category: scams
Subcategory: refund
Summary: Fraudulent charity requests exploiting goodwill and current events to extract donations.
Description: Charity scams impersonate legitimate charitable organizations or create fake causes, especially after natural disasters, during holidays, or for urgent medical needs. Victims are pressured to donate via cash, wire transfer, or gift cards. Little to none of the donated money reaches the claimed cause.
Typical Scenario: "Donate now for hurricane relief" or "Children's cancer fund - urgent need" or "Text-to-donate for disaster victims"
Intent: Financial theft under guise of charitable giving
Behavior: Victim donates money to fake charity; funds misappropriated or kept by scammer
Manipulation Techniques: Empathy (sad story), Urgency (immediate need), Authority (claimed charity status), Social Proof (claimed others are donating)
Common Indicators: Pressure to donate immediately; cash/vote card/gift card requests; no information about how donations are used; unverified charity name; similar-name impersonation of real charities
Legitimate Alternatives: Verified charities through Charity Navigator, GuideStar; direct donations to recognized organizations; checking IRS tax-exempt status
False Positives: Legitimate charities with urgent needs during disasters
False Negatives: Donors who become cynical and miss genuine disaster relief opportunities
Real-world Examples: "Disaster relief fraud," "Fake cancer foundation," "Holiday charity scams," "GoFundMe fraud"
References: FTC Charity Scam Reports; Charity Navigator Scam Alerts; IRS Tax-exempt Organization Search
