Title: Subscription Scam
Category: scams
Subcategory: refund
Summary: Fraudulent subscription services or unauthorized recurring charges.
Description: Subscription scams enroll victims in recurring billing for services they never ordered, didn't knowingly subscribe to, or had hidden terms. Common tactics include free trial auto-enrollment, hidden subscription terms in terms of service, and fake "auto-renewal" notices demanding payment for services not received.
Typical Scenario: "Free 30-day trial" that auto-enrolls at $99/month without clear cancellation; "Outstanding subscription payment" demand for service not used; "Membership auto-renewed" for unrecognized service.
Intent: Recurring financial extraction from victims
Behavior: Victim sees unauthorized charges on statements; may continue for months or years
Manipulation Techniques: Urgency (account will be cancelled), Authority (claimed service provider), Social Proof (claimed "you must have signed up")
Common Indicators: Unrecognized charges on bank/credit card statements; difficulty canceling subscription; hidden terms in lengthy terms of service; auto-renewal without clear opt-out
Legitimate Alternatives: Clear subscription terms; easy cancellation processes; recognized billing descriptors; trial periods with explicit opt-in
False Positives: Legitimate subscriptions user forgot about
False Negatives: Victims who don't review statements regularly
Real-world Examples: "Free trial auto-enrollment," "Fake streaming service bills," "Unrecognized membership fees"
