Title: Crypto Scam
Category: scams
Subcategory: cryptocurrency
Summary: Fraudulent cryptocurrency schemes including fake investments, phishing, and romance scams involving crypto.
Description: Crypto scams encompass fake initial coin offerings (ICOs), fraudulent trading platforms, phishing for private keys, romance scams demanding crypto payments, and "giveaway" scams claiming to multiply sent cryptocurrency. The pseudonymous and irreversible nature of crypto transactions makes recovery nearly impossible.
Typical Scenario: "Send 1 ETH to this address and receive 2 ETH back" or "Verify your wallet - send your private key" or "Romance asks for Bitcoin for emergency"
Intent: Financial theft through cryptocurrency transactions
Behavior: Victim sends crypto to scammer-controlled wallet; funds lost irreversibly
Manipulation Techniques: Greed (investment returns), Urgency (limited time giveaway), Authority (claimed exchange support), Romance (emotional manipulation)
Common Indicators: Requests for private keys; "guaranteed returns"; unsolicited wallet verification; "send crypto to receive more"; addresses not matching official platforms
Legitimate Alternatives: Official exchange platforms; hardware wallets; verified DeFi protocols; self-custody best practices
False Positives: Legitimate crypto promotions with giveaway terms
False Negatives: Victims of sophisticated social engineering in crypto contexts
Real-world Examples: "Fake exchange phishing," "Rug pull" projects, "Romance crypto extortion," "Giveaway scams"
