Title: Fake KYC
Category: scams
Subcategory: banking
Summary: Scammers pose as KYC (Know Your Customer) officials to extract personal and financial information.
Description: Fake KYC scams involve contacting victims claiming their bank account will be suspended unless they complete "Know Your Customer" verification. Victims are directed to provide personal documents, Aadhaar numbers, PAN details, or login credentials to fraudulent websites.
Typical Scenario: "Your KYC is expired. Click here to update immediately or your account will be frozen."
Intent: Personal information and financial data theft
Behavior: Victim provides sensitive documents/credentials to fraudster
Manipulation Techniques: Authority (claimed official status), Fear (account suspension), Urgency (immediate action required)
Common Indicators: Unsolicited KYC update requests; links to non-bank websites; requests for full personal details; pressure to act fast
Legitimate Alternatives: Banks update KYC through official branches or verified app sections; never share full credentials via unsolicited contact
False Positives: Actual KYC updates initiated by the bank
False Negatives: Customers who miss legitimate KYC communications
Real-world Examples: "Update KYC" phishing emails, "Document verification" SMS scams
References: RBI KYC Guidelines; World Bank Financial Inclusion Reports
