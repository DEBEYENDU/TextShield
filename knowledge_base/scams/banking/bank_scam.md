Title: Bank Scam
Category: scams
Subcategory: banking
Summary: Fraudulent communications claiming to be from a bank, aiming to steal account information or money.
Description: Bank scams involve communications appearing to come from a financial institution, claiming there's a problem with the victim's account, unauthorized activity, or that the account will be closed. The goal is to trick victims into revealing login credentials, transferring money, or downloading malware.
Typical Scenario: "Your account has been compromised. Click here to secure it" or "Transfer your funds to a safe account."
Intent: Financial gain and identity theft
Behavior: Victim discloses credentials or transfers money to scammer-controlled account
Manipulation Techniques: Authority (impersonating bank), Urgency (account will be closed), Fear (financial loss)
Common Indicators: Unsolicited communication about account issues; links to non-official domains; requests for PIN or password; poor grammar
Legitimate Alternatives: Banks contacting about genuine issues via official channels; two-factor authentication prompts from your bank
False Positives: Actual bank fraud alerts that happen to be genuine
False Negatives: Victims who ignore legitimate bank warnings
Real-world Examples: "Account blocked" scams, "Unauthorized login" phishing emails
