Title: Technical Support Scam (SMSishing)
Category: scams
Subcategory: smishing
Summary: SMS phishing scams targeting mobile users with fake tech support messages.
Description: Smishing (SMS phishing) scams send text messages claiming the victim's account, payment, or device has issues. Messages include short links to fake pages or phone numbers to call. Common themes include package delivery issues, bank account problems, or subscription renewals requiring immediate action.
Typical Scenario: Text "Your Amazon package is on hold - click to reschedule delivery" or "Bank alert: suspicious login - verify account"
Intent: Credential theft and financial extraction through SMS
Behavior: Victim clicks link or calls number; credentials or money lost
Manipulation Techniques: Urgency (account/issue resolution), Authority (claimed service), Fear (security breach), Curiosity (package/alert)
Common Indicators: Unsolicited text about account/issue; short suspicious link; requests for personal info; unexpected delivery confirmation; grammatical errors
Legitimate Alternatives: Official SMS from services you use; tracking via app; customer service numbers from official sources
False Positives: Legitimate service notifications you've subscribed to
False Negatives: Users who don't recognize smishing among legitimate SMS
Real-world Examples: "Package delivery smishing," "Bank alert smishing," "Subscription renewal smishing"
