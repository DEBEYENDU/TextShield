Title: Technical Support Scam (Vishing)
Category: scams
Subcategory: vishing
Summary: Voice phishing scams where callers impersonate tech support to extract money or information.
Description: Vishing (voice phishing) scams involve unsolicited phone calls claiming the victim's computer, account, or identity has been compromised. The caller may spoof caller ID to appear as a legitimate company or government agency. They pressure victims to grant remote access, provide passwords, or make payments to "fix" the problem.
Typical Scenario: Caller ID shows "Microsoft Security" or "IRS" - "We've detected suspicious activity on your account. Grant remote access immediately."
Intent: Financial theft and unauthorized access through voice communication
Behavior: Victim grants remote access or provides information/payment over the phone
Manipulation Techniques: Authority (claimed agency/company), Fear (compromise/loss), Urgency (immediate action required), Trust Building (known context)
Common Indicators: Unsolicited call about technical/legal issue; request for remote access or payment; caller ID spoofing; pressure to act quickly; poor call quality or foreign accent
Legitimate Alternatives: Genuine tech support from services you've contacted; official agency communications via mail; call-back to verified numbers
False Positives: Actual security alerts from services you use that you've authorized
False Negatives: Victims who don't verify unsolicited call legitimacy
Real-world Examples: "IRS vishing calls," "Microsoft tech support vishing," "Bank fraud vishing"
