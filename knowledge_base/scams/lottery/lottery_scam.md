Title: Lottery Scam
Category: scams
Subcategory: lottery
Summary: Recipients are told they have won a lottery or prize they never entered, requiring payment to claim winnings.
Description: Lottery scams inform victims they have won a large sum of money or a valuable prize in a lottery or sweepstakes they never entered. The victim is told they must pay taxes, fees, or processing charges to claim the winnings. These fees are typically requested via wire transfer, gift cards, or cryptocurrency. Once payment is made, the scammer disappears or invents new fees.
Typical Scenario: An email or call claims "You have won $2 million!" and requests $500 for "tax processing" to release the funds.
Intent: Financial gain through deception
Behavior: Victim pays upfront fees for winnings that don't exist
Manipulation Techniques: Authority (claimed lottery organization), Urgency (limited time to claim), Greed (large financial reward)
Common Indicators: You never entered a lottery; requested payment to claim winnings; pressure to send money quickly via untraceable methods; generic greetings
Legitimate Alternatives: Actual lotteries deduct taxes from winnings; never require upfront payment from winners
False Positives: Legitimate prize promotions where small fees cover shipping/handling
False Negatives: Victims who don't recognize they're being scammed
Real-world Examples: "Spanish Lottery" scams, "Publisher's Clearing House" impersonation
Recommendations: Never pay to claim winnings; verify lottery claims through official channels; remember you can't win a lottery you didn't enter
