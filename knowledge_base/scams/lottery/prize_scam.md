Title: Prize Scam
Category: scams
Subcategory: lottery
Summary: Similar to lottery scams, victims are told they've won a prize requiring payment to claim.
Description: Prize scams notify victims they have won a prize (gift basket, electronics, vacation) in a contest or promotion. Victims are asked to pay shipping, handling, or "processing" fees. Unlike legitimate promotions, real prizes don't require upfront payment.
Typical Scenario: "You've won a free iPhone! Pay $9.99 for shipping."
Intent: Financial gain through deception
Behavior: Victim pays fees for prizes that are never delivered
Manipulation Techniques: Urgency (limited offer), Greed (free valuable item), Authority (claimed partnership with known brand)
Common Indicators: Request for payment to receive prize; prize from contest you didn't enter; pressure to act quickly
Legitimate Alternatives: Legitimate giveaways where winner is selected randomly and no payment is required
False Positives: Legitimate sweepstakes with minimal administrative fees
False Negatives: Victims who misunderstand prize promotion terms
Real-world Examples: "Free gift card" scams, "You've won a free trial" pop-ups
Recommendations: Do not pay fees to claim prizes; verify promotion through official contact information
