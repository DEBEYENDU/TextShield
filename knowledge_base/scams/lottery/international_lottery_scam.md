Title: Lottery Scam (International)
Category: scams
Subcategory: lottery
Summary: International lottery scams targeting victims with foreign lottery claims.
Description: International lottery scams notify victims they've won foreign lotteries (often from countries the victim hasn't visited). The scammer requests payment of taxes, fees, or banking information to process the "winnings." Official cross-border lotteries have strict regulations and never require upfront payment from winners.
Typical Scenario: "You've won the EuroMillions! Pay $800 for tax processing to claim your millions" or "Caribbean lottery winner - provide bank details for transfer."
Intent: Financial gain through cross-border deception
Behavior: Victim pays fees or reveals banking info for non-existent international winnings
Manipulation Techniques: Greed (large international prize), Authority (claimed lottery organization), Urgency (claim before deadline), Fear (tax penalties if not claimed)
Common Indicators: Lottery from country you don't play in; request for payment to claim; requests for bank details; pressure to act quickly; official-looking but non-official documentation
Legitimate Alternatives: Actual lotteries deduct taxes from winnings; never require upfront payment; verify through official lottery organizations
False Positives: Legitimate international lottery promotions with administrative fees (rare)
False Negatives: Victims who don't recognize they can't win lotteries they didn't enter
Real-world Examples: "Foreign lottery win" calls, "Caribbean sweepstakes" emails, "International prize draw" texts
References: FTC International Lottery Scams; Multi-State Lottery Association Guidelines; APWG International Scam Reports
