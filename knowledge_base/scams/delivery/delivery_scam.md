Title: Delivery Scam
Category: scams
Subcategory: delivery
Summary: Fraudulent messages claiming delivery issues or pending packages to extract personal or financial information.
Description: Delivery scams notify victims about a failed or pending package delivery, often claiming a small re-delivery fee or customs duty is required. Victims are led to fraudulent payment sites or tricked into revealing address and payment details.
Typical Scenario: "Package delivery failed - pay $2.99 for re-delivery" or "Customs hold - provide payment info to release parcel."
Intent: Financial theft and personal data collection
Behavior: Victim pays fake fee or reveals payment information
Manipulation Techniques: Authority (claimed courier company), Urgency (package returning to sender), Scarcity (limited delivery window)
Common Indicators: Unexpected package notification; request for payment to release parcel; links to non-courier domains; poor spelling/grammar
Legitimate Alternatives: Genuine delivery notifications from ordered purchases; tracking via carrier's official app
False Positives: Actual delivery issues with legitimate orders
False Negatives: Victims who don't recognize sophisticated courier phishing
Real-world Examples: "FedEx customs scam," "USPS failed delivery" texts
