Title: Courier Scam
Category: scams
Subcategory: delivery
Summary: Scams involving fake courier or logistics companies claiming parcels contain restricted items or require payment.
Description: Courier scams involve calls or messages from fake delivery companies claiming the victim's parcel contains prohibited items, liquids over limits, or restricted goods. The victim is threatened with legal action or asked to pay a "clearance fee" to release the package.
Typical Scenario: "Your parcel contains restricted materials. Pay $50 clearance fee or face prosecution."
Intent: Financial extraction through fear of legal consequences
Behavior: Victim pays fake clearance fee or reveals personal details
Manipulation Techniques: Fear (legal consequences), Authority (claimed customs/courier), Urgency (immediate response required)
Common Indicators: Claims of restricted items in parcel; demand for clearance fee; threats of arrest/prosecution; unsolicited contact
Legitimate Alternatives: Genuine customs issues are handled via official paperwork; couriers don't demand fees for release
False Positives: Actual parcels held for legitimate regulatory reasons
False Negatives: Victims confused by official-looking fraud documents
Real-world Examples: "Package seized by customs" calls, "Drug parcel" extortion scams
References: INTERPOL Illegal Trade Reports; Customs Seizure Scam Alerts
