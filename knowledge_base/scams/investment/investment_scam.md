Title: Investment Scam
Category: scams
Subcategory: investment
Summary: Fraudulent investment opportunities promising high returns with low or no risk.
Description: Investment scams promise guaranteed returns, quick profits, or "too good to be true" opportunities in stocks, real estate, commodities, or investment programs. Victims are persuaded to invest money which is then lost or misappropriated. Common types include Ponzi schemes, pump-and-dump, and fake investment platforms.
Typical Scenario: " guaranteed 20% monthly returns" or "Invest in exclusive crypto opportunity - 50% returns in 30 days" or "Real estate guaranteed flip in 6 months."
Intent: Financial gain for scammer, loss for victim
Behavior: Victim invests money; funds misappropriated or lost
Manipulation Techniques: Greed (high returns), Authority (claimed expertise), Urgency (limited time offer), Social Proof (claimed other investors)
Common Indicators: Guaranteed returns; "too good to be true" rates; pressure to invest quickly; unregistered investment offerings; complex explanations avoidant of questions
Legitimate Alternatives: Registered investment advisors; diversified portfolios; regulated platforms; due diligence on investment opportunities
False Positives: Legitimate high-performing investments (rare, but possible)
False Negatives: Investors who don't recognize sophisticated Ponzi schemes
Real-world Examples: "Ponzi scheme" operations, "Crypto investment platform" fraud, "Real estate seminar" scams
