Title: OTP Scam
Category: scams
Subcategory: otp
Summary: Scammers trick victims into revealing one-time passwords sent to their phones, gaining unauthorized account access.
Description: OTP scams involve contacting victims claiming to be from a bank, tech support, or a service provider, requesting the one-time password sent to their phone under false pretenses (e.g., "to verify your identity," "to fix your account"). Once revealed, the scammer gains full account access.
Typical Scenario: "We've detected suspicious activity. Send me the OTP sent to your phone to verify."
Intent: Unauthorized account access
Behavior: Victim reveals OTP; scammer logs into account
Manipulation Techniques: Authority (impersonating service provider), Urgency (immediate action needed), Trust Building (known service context)
Common Indicators: Request for OTP via phone, text, or email; unsolicited contact about account; pressure to share verification code
Legitimate Alternatives: Services never ask you to share OTPs with anyone; OTPs are for your eyes only
False Positives: Legitimate services requesting OTP for verification (you initiated the action)
False Negatives: Victims who unknowingly share OTPs
Real-world Examples: "Bank OTP verification" calls, "Tech support OTP request" messages
References: NCSC OTP Scam Guidance; Carrier Security Guidelines
