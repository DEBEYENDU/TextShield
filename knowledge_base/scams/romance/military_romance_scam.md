Title: Romance Scam (Military)
Category: scams
Subcategory: romance
Summary: Romance scams specifically impersonating military personnel to extract money from victims.
Description: Military romance scams involve scammers pretending to be deployed service members on dating sites or social media. They build quick romantic relationships, then invent crises related to deployment (leave blocked, medical emergency, equipment needs, transportation home). Victims are asked to send money, gift cards, or cryptocurrency to "help" the supposed service member.
Typical Scenario: "I'm on a peacekeeping mission and can't access my money" or "I need money for emergency leave flight" or "My equipment was damaged in training - send funds for repairs"
Intent: Financial gain through military impersonation and emotional manipulation
Behavior: Victim sends money to supposed deployed service member; relationship ends when money stops
Manipulation Techniques: Trust Building (prolonged grooming), Authority (military status), Urgency (deployment-related crisis), Empathy (concern for service member)
Common Indicators: Claims of military deployment; refusal to video chat or meet; quick declaration of love; requests for money for deployment-related issues; inconsistent rank/unit details
Legitimate Alternatives: Genuine military dating; service members have official leave processes; military financial resources through official channels
False Positives: Relationships where partner is genuinely deployed and may need support (but should go through official channels)
False Negatives: Victims who deny being scammed due to emotional investment in supposed service member
Real-world Examples: "Deployed soldier romance extortion," "Military caregiver scam," "Armed forces dating fraud"
