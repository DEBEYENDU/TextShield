Title: Romance Scam (General)
Category: scams
Subcategory: romance
Summary: Fraudulent romantic relationships used to extract money or personal information from victims.
Description: Romance scams involve building a fake romantic relationship online (dating sites, social media) to gain the victim's trust and affection. Once trust is established, the scammer invents a crisis (medical emergency, travel trouble, business problem) and requests money, often via cryptocurrency or wire transfer. Victims may also be encouraged to involve others.
Typical Scenario: "I love you" after weeks of communication → "I need money for surgery" or "I'm stuck abroad and need bail money" or "Invest in my crypto project"
Intent: Financial gain through emotional manipulation
Behavior: Victim sends money to supposed romantic partner; relationship ends when money stops
Manipulation Techniques: Trust Building (prolonged grooming), Emotional Appeal (love/care), Urgency (crisis requires immediate help), Authority (claimed professional status)
Common Indicators: Refusal to meet in person; quick declaration of love; requests for money for crises; inconsistent stories; profile seems too good to be true
Legitimate Alternatives: Genuine dating relationships; meeting through trusted platforms; getting to know someone before financial entanglement
False Positives: Close relationships where genuine financial support occurs (but should not be coerced or secretive)
False Negatives: Victims who deny being scammed due to emotional investment
Real-world Examples: "Military romance scam," "Travel trapped" extortion, "Crypto romance investment" fraud
