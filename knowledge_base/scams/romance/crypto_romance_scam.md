Title: Romance Scam (Crypto Extension)
Category: scams
Subcategory: romance
Summary: Romance scams that transition into cryptocurrency investment fraud.
Description: After building a romantic relationship, the scammer introduces the victim to "profitable" cryptocurrency investment opportunities or asks the victim to invest in their crypto project. The scammer may show fake returns initially, then disappear with all funds, or encourage the victim to invest more until the platform collapses.
Typical Scenario: "I made huge profits with Bitcoin - would you like to invest?" or "Join my crypto project - guaranteed returns" or "Let me show you how to trade crypto"
Intent: Financial theft through cryptocurrency combined with romance manipulation
Behavior: Victim invests crypto; funds lost when scammer disappears or platform collapses
Manipulation Techniques: Trust Building (romantic relationship), Greed (investment returns), Authority (claimed crypto expertise), Urgency (limited time offer)
Common Indicators: Introduction of crypto investment early in relationship; guarantees of returns; requests to transfer crypto to "wallet"; pressure to invest more; complex explanations avoiding questions
Legitimate Alternatives: Verified crypto exchanges; regulated investment platforms; independent financial advice; self-education on crypto fundamentals
False Positives: Genuine romantic partners who share investment interests (but should not pressure or combine finances prematurely)
False Negatives: Victims who don't recognize crypto scam within established relationship
Real-world Examples: "Crypto romance pyramid," "Partnership investment scam," "Trading algorithm fraud"
