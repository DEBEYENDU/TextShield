Title: Income Tax Scam
Category: scams
Subcategory: government
Summary: Scammers fraudulently claim tax debts or refunds to extract money or information from victims.
Description: Income tax scams can take two forms: (1) victims are told they owe back taxes and face penalties/arrest if not paid immediately, or (2) victims are told they're due a fake refund and must provide bank details or pay "processing fees" to receive it. The IRS and tax authorities never initiate contact via phone/email for tax demands or refunds out of the blue.
Typical Scenario: "You're due a $1,500 tax refund - provide your bank details and SSN to claim it" or "You owe $2,000 in back taxes - pay via wire transfer or face deportation."
Intent: Financial theft and identity extraction
Behavior: Victim provides sensitive tax information or pays fake tax debts
Manipulation Techniques: Fear (legal consequences), Greed (fake refund), Authority (claimed IRS/tax office)
Common Indicators: Unsolicited tax communication; requests for SSN/bank details; pressure to pay via unconventional methods; threats of arrest/deportation
Legitimate Alternatives: Official IRS communications via mail; tax refunds deposited to known accounts; verified tax portal access
False Positives: Actual IRS notices received through official mail
False Negatives: Taxpayers who miss legitimate IRS correspondence
Real-world Examples: "Refund processing fee" scams, "Taxpayer compliance" extortion
References: IRS Tax Scams Consumer Guide; FTC Tax-Related Scam Data
