Title: Placement Announcements
Category: legitimate
Subcategory: educational
Summary: Genuine campus placement announcements from university placement cells about recruiter visits, eligibility and schedules.
Description: Placement announcements inform students about companies visiting campus, roles offered, eligibility criteria, CTC details and selection schedules. They come from official placement cell addresses, never ask for fees to attend, and direct students to campus venues or official portals.
Typical Scenario: "Placement drive by Infosys on 12th March" or "Congratulations to placed students" or "Pre-placement talk schedule" or "Eligibility list for TCS Ninja drive"
Intent: Inform students and congratulate placed candidates
Behavior: Recipient attends drives or talks; no payment or credential sharing involved
Manipulation Techniques: None (legitimate institutional communication)
Common Indicators: Official college domain; named recruiter companies; venue on campus; placement officer signature; no registration fee; no OTP or password request
Legitimate Alternatives: None - this is the legitimate communication itself
False Positives: Placement mails flagged because they contain "offer", "selected" or salary figures
False Negatives: Students missing drives due to spam-folder delivery
Real-world Examples: "Campus drive: Wipro Project Engineer", "Pre-placement talk by Deloitte", "Congratulations CSE batch placed in Accenture", "Internship drive by Flipkart"
References: University Placement Policies; AICTE Placement Guidelines
