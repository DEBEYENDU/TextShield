Title: Salary Credit and HR Communication
Category: legitimate
Subcategory: corporate
Summary: Genuine salary credit alerts, payslip notifications and HR announcements sent by employers and banks.
Description: Salary communications state the credited amount, employer name, account reference and payslip availability. They never ask employees to verify bank details over SMS, pay release fees, or share OTPs. HR notices about holidays, appraisals and policies come from official HR addresses.
Typical Scenario: "Salary credited Rs.85,000" or "Payslip for August available" or "Holiday list 2026" or "Appraisal cycle starts"
Intent: Notify employees of payments and company updates
Behavior: Employee checks bank balance or HR portal; no action on links required
Manipulation Techniques: None (employer payroll workflow)
Common Indicators: Employer name present; matches expected salary date; no fee or OTP request; links only to known HR portal; appraisal language formal
Legitimate Alternatives: None - this is the legitimate communication itself
False Positives: Salary alerts flagged because they contain money amounts and "credited"
False Negatives: Employees missing appraisal deadlines
Real-world Examples: "Salary credit from TCS", "Payslip uploaded on Darwinbox", "Diwali bonus credited", "Form 16 available"
References: Company Payroll Policies; Labour Law Disclosures
