Title: Bank Notifications
Category: legitimate
Subcategory: communication
Summary: Official notifications from financial institutions regarding account activity, updates, or required actions.
Description: Legitimate bank notifications inform customers about account activity, security updates, policy changes, or required actions. These communications come through official channels (email, SMS, app notifications) and contain verifiable information about the customer's actual account. Banks never request sensitive information like full PINs or passwords via these notifications.
Typical Scenario: "Your deposit of $500 has been completed" or "Your card was used at 2:30 PM in City Center" or "Your 2FA code is 483921"
Intent: Inform customers of account activity and security
Behavior: Customer receives informational communication about their actual account
Manipulation Techniques: None (legitimate communication provides factual information)
Common Indicators: Comes from official bank domain/number; relates to actual account activity; no request for sensitive information; contains verifiable transaction details
Legitimate Alternatives: None - this is the legitimate communication itself
False Positives: Legitimate fraud alerts that happen to be genuine security notifications
False Negatives: Customers who ignore or miss important bank communications
Real-world Examples: "Direct deposit received" SMS, "Card transaction alert" email, "Monthly statement available" app notification
References: FDIC Consumer Compliance; OCC Bank Notification Guidelines; FTC Financial Fraud Prevention
