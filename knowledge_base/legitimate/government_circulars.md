Title: Government Circulars and Advisories
Category: legitimate
Subcategory: government
Summary: Official circulars, public notices and citizen advisories issued by ministries, departments and civic bodies.
Description: Government advisories announce schemes, deadlines, helplines and public notices on official letterheads with circular numbers. They never demand instant payment over SMS links, never ask for OTPs, and direct citizens to .gov.in portals or toll-free helplines. Payments happen only at official counters or portals.
Typical Scenario: "Property tax deadline extended" or "Voter list verification camp" or "Water supply maintenance notice" or "Scholarship scheme announced"
Intent: Inform citizens and warn about genuine civic matters
Behavior: Citizen visits official portal or office; verifies circular number if unsure
Manipulation Techniques: None (official public communication)
Common Indicators: Department name and circular number; .gov.in references; toll-free helpline; no shortened links; no OTP demand; formal gazette language
Legitimate Alternatives: None - this is the legitimate communication itself
False Positives: Real advisories flagged because they mention "penalty", "deadline" or "verify"
False Negatives: Citizens missing tax deadlines or scheme benefits
Real-world Examples: "MCD property tax notice", "Passport seva appointment", "EPFO interest credited", "Election voter slip"
References: Government of India Communication Manual; Press Information Bureau
