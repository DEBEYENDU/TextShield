Title: Business Communication
Category: legitimate
Subcategory: communication
Summary: Standard professional business communications between organizations and clients/partners.
Description: Business communications encompass invoices, proposals, contracts, updates, and professional correspondence. These come from verified business entities, contain accurate organizational information, and serve legitimate commercial or professional purposes. Legitimate business communications respect professional norms and privacy.
Typical Scenario: "Invoice #8932 for services rendered" or "Proposal for partnership collaboration" or "Quarterly business update" or "Contract renewal notice"
Intent: Conduct legitimate business operations and professional communication
Behavior: Recipient receives genuine business communication; can process, respond, or file as appropriate
Manipulation Techniques: None (legitimate communication serves business purpose)
Common Indicators: Comes from verified business domain/organization; contains accurate company information; serves legitimate commercial purpose; follows professional norms; no urgency pressure for unreasonable actions
Legitimate Alternatives: None - this is the legitimate communication itself
False Positives: Legitimate business communications from various partnerships and affiliations the recipient is part of
False Negatives: Recipients who overlook important business updates or opportunities
Real-world Examples: "Monthly invoice," "Project proposal," "Partnership agreement," "Business update newsletter"
References: Business communication standards; FTC Commercial Communication Guidelines; ICC Business Protocols
