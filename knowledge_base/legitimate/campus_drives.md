Title: Campus Drives and Recruitment Notices
Category: legitimate
Subcategory: recruitment
Summary: Authentic recruiter communications about walk-in interviews, shortlisting, offer letters and joining formalities.
Description: Recruitment notices from HR departments describe open roles, interview venues, required documents and contact persons. Genuine recruiters never ask for registration fees, security deposits or OTPs over SMS. Interviews happen at offices or campuses, and offer letters come on company letterhead.
Typical Scenario: "Walk-in interview for customer support roles" or "You are shortlisted for round 2" or "Offer letter from TechMahindra" or "Joining date confirmation"
Intent: Recruit candidates and coordinate interview logistics
Behavior: Candidate attends interview with resume and ID proof; no advance payment
Manipulation Techniques: None (legitimate hiring workflow)
Common Indicators: Company domain email; named interviewer; office address; job description attached; no fee demand; no work-from-home advance payment
Legitimate Alternatives: None - this is the legitimate communication itself
False Positives: Genuine job mails flagged because they contain "earn", "apply now" or salary numbers
False Negatives: Candidates ignoring real offers fearing job scams
Real-world Examples: "Shortlisted for HDFC Bank teller interview", "Walk-in: BPO roles in Noida", "Offer letter: Junior Accountant", "Campus recruitment: L&T"
References: Company HR Policies; Labour Department Advisories
