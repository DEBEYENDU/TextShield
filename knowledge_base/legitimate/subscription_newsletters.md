Title: Subscriptions and Newsletters
Category: legitimate
Subcategory: communication
Summary: Genuine subscription renewals, plan receipts and opted-in newsletters with manage-preference options.
Description: Subscription messages confirm plan names, billing cycles, amounts and renewal dates, and always offer cancellation or preference management. Newsletters arrive because the user subscribed, carry the publisher name, and include an unsubscribe path. Neither asks for credentials over SMS.
Typical Scenario: "Netflix plan renewed" or "Weekly tech newsletter" or "Trial ends in 3 days" or "Premium receipt"
Intent: Confirm renewals and deliver subscribed content
Behavior: Subscriber manages the plan in account settings or unsubscribes freely
Manipulation Techniques: None (opted-in commercial relationship)
Common Indicators: Matches a service the user holds; plan name and dates; manage or unsubscribe option; no credential request; sender matches the brand
Legitimate Alternatives: None - this is the legitimate communication itself
False Positives: Real renewals flagged because they contain "expiring", "offer" or "upgrade"
False Negatives: Users missing trial-end billing
Real-world Examples: "Spotify Premium receipt", "Morning Context newsletter", "iCloud storage plan", "Gym membership renewal"
References: TRAI Commercial Communication Rules; IT Act Spam Provisions
