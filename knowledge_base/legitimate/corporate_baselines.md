Title: Corporate Communication Baselines
Category: legitimate
Summary: Genuine corporate messages are structured, attributable, and verifiable, providing a contrast baseline for manipulation detection.
Description: Legitimate corporate communication carries sender identity, subject context, greeting and closing, reference numbers where relevant, and requests that match known workflows. It tolerates delays and verification callbacks. Deviations (secrecy, personal channels, urgency) are themselves signals.
Typical Scenario: "Meeting invite with agenda" or "Appraisal cycle notice" or "Policy update circular" or "Payslip available"
Purpose: Baseline for recognizing professional versus manipulative style
Detection Signals: named sender and department; greeting and closing; workflow-consistent requests; no secrecy; verification welcomed
Legitimate Uses: Everyday HR, finance, and operations communication
Examples: "HR appraisal notice," "Finance policy update," "Team meeting invite"
Risk Level: Low - this is the legitimate baseline itself
