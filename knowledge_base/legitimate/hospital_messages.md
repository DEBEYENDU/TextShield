Title: Hospital Messages
Category: legitimate
Subcategory: communication
Summary: Official communications from healthcare providers regarding patient care, appointments, or health information.
Description: Hospital and healthcare communications inform patients about appointments, test results, treatment updates, or preventive care. These come from verified healthcare providers and protect patient privacy (HIPAA compliance). Legitimate healthcare messages never request payment or sensitive information via insecure channels.
Typical Scenario: "Your appointment is tomorrow at 2 PM" or "Lab results ready - provider will contact you" or "Flu shot clinic available" or "Prescription ready for pickup"
Intent: Inform patients of health-related matters and care updates
Behavior: Patient receives genuine health communication from verified provider
Manipulation Techniques: None (legitimate communication supports patient care)
Common Indicators: Comes from verified healthcare provider; relates to actual patient care; HIPAA-compliant; no request for payment via insecure channel; provides specific health information
Legitimate Alternatives: None - this is the legitimate communication itself
False Positives: Legitimate health reminders that patients may have scheduled
False Negatives: Patients who miss important health communications or appointments
Real-world Examples: "Appointment reminder" text, "Lab results notification" email, "Medication refill available" call, "Preventive care reminders"
References: HIPAA Privacy Rules; Healthcare compliance guidelines; FTC Health Scam Prevention
