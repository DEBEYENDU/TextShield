Title: Tax and Legal Notices
Category: legitimate
Subcategory: government
Summary: Authentic income-tax intimations, GST notices and legal summons served through official portals and registered post.
Description: Tax and legal communications cite assessment years, PAN, notice sections and document submission windows on the income-tax or GST portal. They never demand immediate transfer to personal accounts, never ask for OTPs or passwords, and always reference verifiable case numbers checkable on official sites.
Typical Scenario: "ITR processed, refund issued" or "GST return due date" or "Court hearing notice" or "Form 26AS available"
Intent: Notify taxpayers of filings, refunds and hearings
Behavior: Taxpayer logs into the official portal to verify and respond
Manipulation Techniques: None (statutory communication)
Common Indicators: PAN masked correctly; assessment year stated; incometax.gov.in reference; response window of weeks; no personal-account payment; no OTP demand
Legitimate Alternatives: None - this is the legitimate communication itself
False Positives: Real intimations flagged because they contain "penalty", "scrutiny" or "pay tax"
False Negatives: Taxpayers missing filing deadlines
Real-world Examples: "ITR-V acknowledgement", "Refund credited by ITD", "GST GSTR-1 due", "E-court hearing SMS"
References: Income Tax Act Procedures; CBIC GST Guidelines
