Title: Delivery Updates
Category: legitimate
Subcategory: communication
Summary: Genuine delivery notifications from carriers and retailers about ordered packages.
Description: Legitimate delivery updates inform customers about the status of packages they have ordered. These include order confirmations, shipping notifications, delivery windows, and delivery completion messages. Customers can track orders via the carrier's official tracking system.
Typical Scenario: "Your order #12345 has shipped" or "Package out for delivery today between 2-4 PM" or "Delivery completed - signature required" or "Delivery exception - weather delay"
Intent: Inform customers of ordered package status
Behavior: Customer receives status updates about genuinely ordered items
Manipulation Techniques: None (legitimate communication provides ordered service updates)
Common Indicators: Relates to actual ordered items; provides tracking number; matches carrier's official system; delivery address matches customer; no request for payment or personal information
Legitimate Alternatives: None - this is the legitimate communication itself
False Positives: Legitimate delivery updates for orders the customer placed but may have forgotten
False Negatives: Customers who don't track orders and miss delivery windows
Real-world Examples: "Amazon shipping notification," "FedEx delivery update," "USPS tracking alert," "Retail order confirmation"
References: Carrier service guides; E-commerce best practices; FTC Online Shopping Protections
