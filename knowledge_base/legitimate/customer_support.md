Title: Customer Support
Category: legitimate
Subcategory: communication
Summary: Legitimate customer service communications from businesses assisting customers.
Description: Customer support communications help customers with inquiries, issues, or requests. These come through official support channels (email, phone, chat, ticket systems) and address genuine customer needs. Legitimate support respects customer privacy and doesn't pressure for immediate decisions or payments.
Typical Scenario: "Your support ticket #5678 has been addressed" or "We're following up on your recent inquiry" or "Your return has been processed" or "Account update: password reset confirmed"
Intent: Assist customers with issues, inquiries, or requests
Behavior: Customer receives genuine support response; issue addressed or inquiry handled
Manipulation Techniques: None (legitimate communication serves customer service purpose)
Common Indicators: Relates to actual support ticket or inquiry; comes from official support channel; addresses specific issue; no pressure for immediate action; provides resolution steps or next steps
Legitimate Alternatives: None - this is the legitimate communication itself
False Positives: Legitimate follow-up emails from businesses you've recently contacted
False Negatives: Customers who don't check support channels and miss issue resolutions
Real-world Examples: "Ticket resolved" email, "Order status update," "Return confirmation," "Survey invitation for recent purchase"
References: Customer Service best practices; FTC Consumer Rights; Better Business Bureau Complaint Guidelines
