Title: Telecom Recharge and Bills
Category: legitimate
Subcategory: telecom
Summary: Genuine mobile recharge confirmations, data balance alerts, DTH renewals and broadband bills from operators.
Description: Telecom messages state the recharged amount, plan validity, data balance and operator name, matching the user's SIM operator. They never ask for OTPs to keep the number active, never threaten instant disconnection with a pay-now link, and point to the operator app or USSD for balance checks.
Typical Scenario: "Recharge of Rs.239 successful" or "Data balance 1.2GB" or "DTH recharge due" or "Broadband bill generated"
Intent: Confirm recharges and remind of plan expiry
Behavior: Subscriber checks balance on the operator app; recharges through known channels
Manipulation Techniques: None (operator billing workflow)
Common Indicators: Matches the user's operator; amount and validity stated; no OTP demand; no same-hour disconnection threat; operator sender ID
Legitimate Alternatives: None - this is the legitimate communication itself
False Positives: Real recharge alerts flagged because they contain "expired", "recharge now" or "blocked"
False Negatives: Users missing plan expiry
Real-world Examples: "Jio Rs.239 recharge", "Airtel data balance", "Tata Play renewal", "BSNL validity alert"
References: TRAI Tariff Regulations; Operator Billing Codes
