Title: Insurance and Utility Bills
Category: legitimate
Subcategory: finance
Summary: Genuine premium receipts, policy renewals, electricity, water and gas bill notifications from providers.
Description: Insurance and utility communications state policy or consumer numbers, amounts, due dates and official payment channels (provider app, website or authorized centers). They never threaten same-day disconnection with a pay-now link, never ask for OTPs, and reference the consumer ID the user already holds.
Typical Scenario: "LIC premium receipt" or "Electricity bill Rs.1,240 due" or "Policy renewal reminder" or "Gas cylinder delivered"
Intent: Remind customers of dues and confirm payments
Behavior: Customer pays through the provider app or billing counter before the due date
Manipulation Techniques: None (provider billing workflow)
Common Indicators: Consumer or policy number matches; provider name; due date in future; official payment channels; no same-day threats; no OTP requests
Legitimate Alternatives: None - this is the legitimate communication itself
False Positives: Real bills flagged because they mention "due", "disconnection" or "pay now"
False Negatives: Customers missing renewal dates
Real-world Examples: "BSES electricity bill", "LIC premium due", "Indane refill delivered", "HDFC Ergo renewal"
References: IRDAI Policyholder Guidelines; State Electricity Regulations
