Title: Appointment Reminders
Category: legitimate
Subcategory: communication
Summary: Official appointment reminders from healthcare, service, or professional providers.
Description: Legitimate appointment reminders notify patients/clients of upcoming appointments. These include date, time, location (or virtual link), and often allow rescheduling or cancellation. Reminders come from verified providers and respect patient/client scheduling preferences.
Typical Scenario: "Reminder: Your appointment is tomorrow at 3 PM" or "Your dental cleaning is scheduled for Friday" or "Your consultation call is Monday at 10 AM via Zoom" or "Your vehicle service appointment - Tuesday at 9 AM"
Intent: Help recipients remember and attend scheduled appointments
Behavior: Recipient receives reminder; can attend, reschedule, or cancel as needed
Manipulation Techniques: None (legitimate communication supports appointment adherence)
Common Indicators: Relates to actual scheduled appointment; provides specific details (date/time); comes from verified provider; allows rescheduling/cancellation; matches previous confirmation
Legitimate Alternatives: None - this is the legitimate communication itself
False Positives: Legitimate reminders for appointments the recipient scheduled but may have forgotten
False Negatives: Recipients who miss reminders and miss appointments
Real-world Examples: "Dental appointment reminder," "Doctor's visit notification," "Salon booking reminder," "Consultation call reminder"
References: Healthcare appointment guidelines; Service provider policies; FTC Consumer Appointment Rights
