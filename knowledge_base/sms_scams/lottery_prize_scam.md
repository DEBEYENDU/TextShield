# Lottery and prize scams

"Congratulations, you have won..." is the oldest and most persistent scam template in existence. It is an advance-fee fraud: a large reward is promised, and the victim pays small fees to release it - fees that keep growing until the victim stops or is broke.

## Anatomy of a prize scam message

1. **Unexpected win** - "You have won Rs.50,000 / an iPhone / a Dubai trip".
2. **Small barrier** - "pay the processing fee", "shipping charges", "tax", "registration fee", "courier charges".
3. **Time pressure** - "claim within 2 hours", "final one-hour window".
4. **Endless fees** - after the first payment, more fees appear: insurance, conversion fees, customs.
5. **Disappearance** - the caller/writer vanishes after the money is sent.

## Common prize scam phrasings

- "You have been selected for a free gift"
- "Your mobile number has won a lucky draw"
- "Claim your reward before the offer expires"
- "Pay Rs.499 courier charges to receive your free watch"
- "You are the 100th customer - win a gold coin"

## Why it is always a scam

Real lotteries and promotions never ask winners to pay anything to receive winnings. If a message congratulates you on a win you never entered, and then asks for money - even a small amount - it is fraud by construction. TextShield marks prize/lottery language as high severity, and the risk engine escalates when a fee is requested alongside it.

## Recommended action

Ignore the message. Do not call the number, do not share details, do not pay any fee. If a friend or relative forwards such a message, warn them as well.
