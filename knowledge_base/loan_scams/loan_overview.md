# Loan scams

Loan scams target financial distress: they offer instant loans with no documents, no credit check, and no collateral - and then charge the victim to receive the money.

## Standard loan scam template

- Bait: "Instant loan up to Rs.5,00,000. No documents, no CIBIL, low EMI."
- Contact: victim provides personal details via the message link or form.
- Hook: "Congratulations! Your loan is sanctioned. Pay the processing fee of Rs.1,500 to release the amount." OR "Your loan is approved but needs insurance of Rs.800."
- Repeat: another 'fee' (GST, disbursement charge, first EMI) before any money arrives.
- Outcome: fees paid, loan never disbursed, victim data harvested.

## Variants

- **Pre-approved loan** - "You are pre-approved for Rs.3 lakhs from XYZ Finance. Activate within 24 hours."
- **Loan against EMI clearance** - paid 'credit score repair' packages that do nothing.
- **Government scheme loans** - fake PMMY/PMJDY scheme loans with 'processing charges' paid to a private account.

## Legitimate contrast

Real lenders: verify documents, run credit checks, sign contracts, disburse to the borrower's own account, and deduct fees from disbursement - they never ask for advance payments to activate a loan. A message that gates a loan behind an upfront 'processing fee' is the defining fraud pattern.

## Detection notes

Loan vocabulary (instant loan, approved, sanction, EMI, processing fee, no CIBIL) is scored medium-high. Coupled with a payment request the message becomes HIGH risk. The recommended action: report to cyber cell and the RBI Sachet portal for unregistered lenders; block the sender.
