Title: False Reassurance Tactics
Category: manipulation
Summary: Scammers neutralize suspicion with absolute safety claims, fake certifications, and premature trust language.
Description: False reassurance appears as 100% safe guarantees, invented registrations (RBI approved, ISO certified), and demands for blind trust. Legitimate institutions state verifiable license numbers, publish terms, and never ask for blind trust or secrecy.
Typical Scenario: "100% safe, RBI registered" or "Fully secure, just trust us" or "No risk, guaranteed"
Purpose: Flag safety theater used to suppress verification
Detection Signals: absolute guarantees; unverifiable certifications; blind-trust demands; secrecy paired with safety claims
Legitimate Uses: Licensed institutions citing checkable registration numbers and terms
Examples: "Fake RBI-registered lender," "Guaranteed-return trading app," "No-risk crypto doubling"
Risk Level: High - directly disables victim skepticism
References: RBI Fraud Advisories; FTC Guarantee Fraud Data
