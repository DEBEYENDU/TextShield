Title: Recruitment Pattern Manipulation
Category: manipulation
Summary: Fake recruiters exploit job seekers with advance-fee, task, and credential-harvesting schemes.
Description: Recruitment manipulation mimics hiring workflows (interviews, offer letters) but inserts payment demands, paid starter tasks, or data collection. Genuine employers never charge joining fees, never pay wages before work via task apps, and interview through verifiable company channels.
Typical Scenario: "Pay registration fee for selection" or "Complete paid tasks on the app" or "Share OTP to confirm offer"
Purpose: Separate real hiring from job-scam workflows
Detection Signals: joining or training fee; task-app wages; OTP or bank detail collection; personal email contact; no verifiable office
Legitimate Uses: Real campus drives and HR interviews with on-site or official-domain processes
Examples: "Task scam on Telegram," "Fake work-from-home fee," "Bogus overseas placement"
Risk Level: High - targets financially vulnerable seekers
References: Labour Department Advisories; FTC Job Scam Data
