Title: Educational
Category: communication_styles
Summary: Informative communication designed to teach, explain, or convey knowledge.
Description: Educational communication focuses on clarity, accuracy, and learning facilitation. It uses structured explanations, examples, and often assesses understanding. Educational communication may range from formal lessons to casual tips, always aiming to increase recipient knowledge or skills.
Typical Scenario: Tutorials, how-to guides, lesson content, educational emails, training materials, explanatory articles
Purpose: Facilitate learning; convey accurate information; increase knowledge or skills; support education or training goals
Detection Indicators: Clear learning objectives or topics; structured explanation; examples or illustrations; may include assessments or checkpoints; accurate, verifiable information; focus on understanding rather than action or purchase
Legitimate Uses: School lessons; training materials; how-to guides; educational content; tutorials; informational resources
Examples: "How to format a document" "Step-by-step guide to..." "Learn about..." "Understanding the basics of..." "Here's what you need to know..."
Risk Level: Low - educational communication is recognizable and rarely used in scams (scammers prefer persuasive or manipulative styles)
