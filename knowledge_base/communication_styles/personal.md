Title: Personal
Category: communication_styles
Summary: Communication focused on individual relationships, feelings, or personal matters.
Description: Personal communication addresses individual experiences, emotions, relationships, or private matters. It varies widely in tone and structure based on the relationship and context, but always centers on the individual or small group rather than organizational or formal purposes.
Typical Scenario: Messages to family and friends, personal updates, life events, personal questions, intimate communication, relationship communication
Purpose: Maintain personal connections; share individual experiences; express emotions; support personal relationships; communicate personal matters
Detection Indicators: Individual-centered content; personal details; emotional or relational focus; varied tone based on relationship; often lacks organizational structure; may include personal anecdotes or updates
Legitimate Uses: Family communication; friend interactions; personal updates; life event sharing; relationship communication; personal advice or support
Examples: "How have you been?" "Congratulations on your achievement!" "Thinking of you during this" "Sharing my experience with..." "What are your thoughts on..."
Risk Level: Low to Medium - personal communication is recognizable but can be exploited in relationship-targeted scams (romance, family impersonation)
