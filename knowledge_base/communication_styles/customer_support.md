Title: Customer Support
Category: communication_styles
Summary: Help-focused communication assisting customers with issues, inquiries, or requests.
Description: Customer support communication prioritizes helpfulness, clarity, and problem-resolution. It employs empathetic language, clear troubleshooting steps, and offers legitimate assistance channels. Support communication respects customer time and addresses genuine concerns without pressure or manipulation.
Typical Scenario: Support ticket responses, help desk replies, live chat assistance, phone support interactions, follow-up communications after issue resolution
Purpose: Resolve customer issues; answer inquiries; provide assistance; maintain customer satisfaction; build loyalty
Detection Indicators: Help-oriented language; specific issue address; troubleshooting steps or solutions; offers legitimate support channels; no pressure for immediate decisions; respects customer autonomy; provides clear next steps or escalation options
Legitimate Uses: Genuine customer service; support ticket resolution; help desk assistance; query resolution; post-interaction follow-up
Examples: "We've resolved your issue..." "Here are steps to fix..." "Is there anything else I can help with?" "Your ticket #1234 has been addressed"
Risk Level: Low - genuine customer support is easily distinguished from scams; distinction in channel, tone, and intent
References: Customer service best practices; FTC Consumer rights; BBB complaint resolution guidelines
