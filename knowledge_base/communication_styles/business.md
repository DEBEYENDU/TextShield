Title: Business
Category: communication_styles
Summary: Professional communication focused on business purposes and organizational goals.
Description: Business communication combines elements of formality with practical efficiency. It uses professional language suited to corporate contexts while maintaining clarity and directness. Business communication serves organizational objectives, facilitates transactions, and supports professional relationships.
Typical Scenario: Email about project updates, meeting agendas, sales proposals, business inquiries, performance reports, client communications
Purpose: Facilitate business operations; support professional relationships; communicate organizational information; enable transactions and decisions
Detection Indicators: Professional but not overly formal tone; clear purpose or request; business-related content; organizational context; may include references, deadlines, action items; often sent during business hours
Legitimate Uses: Corporate communication; business proposals; project updates; client relations; sales and marketing within business context; internal organizational communication
Examples: "Following up on our discussion..." "Please find the attached proposal..." "Per our meeting..." "Regarding the Q3 report..." "Action items from our call..."
Risk Level: Low to Medium - business communication is common but can be spoofed in business-targeted scams
