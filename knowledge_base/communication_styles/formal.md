Title: Formal
Category: communication_styles
Summary: Professional, structured communication following established protocols and conventions.
Description: Formal communication uses precise language, proper grammar, structured format, and follows professional conventions. It is used in official business, legal, academic, and government contexts. Formal communication avoids slang, colloquialisms, and emotional language, focusing on clarity, objectivity, and professionalism.
Typical Scenario: Official business letters, legal documents, government correspondence, academic publications, formal reports
Purpose: Convey professionalism; ensure clarity in important matters; maintain protocols and records; demonstrate respect for recipient and subject
Detection Indicators: Proper salutation and closure; structured format (introduction, body, conclusion); precise language; no slang or colloquialisms; formal tone throughout; often includes reference numbers, dates, official headers
Legitimate Uses: Business contracts, legal notices, official government correspondence, academic submissions, formal complaints
Examples: "Dear Sir/Madam," "Please find attached..." "Sincerely," "Formal business proposal" "Official notice of..."
Risk Level: Low - formal communication is easily recognizable and rarely used in scams (scammers prefer informal or semi-formal)
