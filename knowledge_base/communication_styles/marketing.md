Title: Marketing
Category: communication_styles
Summary: Persuasive communication designed to promote products, services, or ideas.
Description: Marketing communication uses compelling language, visual elements, and psychological techniques to influence recipient behavior or perceptions. It focuses on value propositions, benefits, and calls to action. Marketing may use various tones from formal to informal depending on target audience and channel, but always serves promotional goals.
Typical Scenario: Advertisements, promotional emails, campaign announcements, product launches, sales offers, brand messaging
Purpose: Promote products or services; influence recipient behavior; build brand awareness; generate leads or sales
Detection Indicators: Clear promotional intent; calls to action; benefits-focused language; may include discounts, offers, limited-time deals; often uses psychological techniques (urgency, scarcity, social proof); may require opt-in
Legitimate Uses: Product promotions; brand advertising; campaign announcements; sales events; legitimate marketing communications recipients have opted into
Examples: "limited time offer 50% off!" "New product launch - be first to know!" "Exclusive deal for subscribers" "Introducing our latest innovation"
Risk Level: Medium - marketing communication is common and can overlap with scam tactics; distinction often in opt-in status and transparency
References: Marketing communication standards; Advertising ethics; FTC Marketing and advertising guidelines
