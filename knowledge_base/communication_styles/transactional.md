Title: Transactional
Category: communication_styles
Summary: Communication focused on specific transactions, exchanges, or functional purposes.
Description: Transactional communication serves immediate functional needs - confirming exchanges, providing necessary information, completing processes, or documenting actions. It is practical, direct, and focused on the transaction at hand rather than relationship-building or persuasion.
Typical Scenario: Order confirmations, receipt notifications, shipping updates, appointment confirmations, payment confirmations, service activations
Purpose: Complete or document specific transactions; provide necessary transaction information; confirm actions taken; support process completion
Detection Indicators: Specific transaction reference; functional content; necessary details (amounts, dates, references); direct and concise; no persuasive language; confirms or documents exchange
Legitimate Uses: Order confirmations; receipt notifications; appointment confirmations; payment receipts; service activations; process completions
Examples: "Your order #12345 confirmed" "Receipt for your purchase" "Appointment confirmed for Tuesday" "Payment received - thank you" "Service activated successfully"
Risk Level: Low to Medium - transactional communication is common and can be spoofed; distinction in accuracy of details and source verification
References: E-commerce best practices; Transaction communication standards; FTC Transaction communication guidelines
