Title: FOMO and Loss Aversion
Category: psychology
Summary: Fear of missing out and overweighting losses push victims into rushed financial decisions.
Description: Scammers frame offers as expiring and inaction as loss, exploiting the bias that losses hurt more than equal gains please. Countdowns, limited slots, and today-only bonuses are the standard toolkit. Legitimate promotions allow comparison time and clear terms.
Typical Scenario: "Offer ends tonight" or "Only 3 slots left" or "You will lose your bonus"
Purpose: Recognize manufactured scarcity driving rushed payments
Detection Signals: countdown pressure; loss-framed language; no time to compare; bonus for immediate payment
Legitimate Uses: Genuine limited sales with published terms and return policies
Examples: "Fake investment slots," "Countdown gift-card scam," "Bonus expiry fraud"
Risk Level: Medium - effective mainly with reward framing
References: Kahneman Loss Aversion Research; FTC Prize Fraud Data
