Title: Guilt and Sympathy Exploitation
Category: psychology
Summary: Attackers pose as distressed relatives, colleagues, or causes to extract money through guilt and sympathy.
Description: Sympathy scams present a suffering persona (stranded traveler, sick relative, charity case) and request urgent help that bypasses verification. Guilt variants invoke obligation after fabricated favors. Genuine appeals accept verification and never demand secrecy or instant transfers.
Typical Scenario: "Stranded abroad, please wire money" or "After all we did for you" or "Donate now, child in ICU"
Purpose: Separate emotional manipulation from genuine distress
Detection Signals: unverifiable identity; secrecy request; instant transfer demand; refusal of video call; new contact channel
Legitimate Uses: Verified charity drives and known contacts reachable independently
Examples: "Stranded traveler scam," "Romance scam money request," "Fake charity collection"
Risk Level: Medium - potent against empathetic targets
References: FTC Romance and Charity Fraud Reports
